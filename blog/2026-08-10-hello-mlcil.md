This site is where our work now lives: machine learning for molecules, the
question of whether any of it actually works, and the software we write along
the way.

<!-- truncate -->

Three places to start.

[Publications](/publications) has everything we have published, from graph
classification baselines to peptide function prediction and pesticide
ecotoxicology. Each entry links to the paper, the preprint and the code.

[Software and datasets](/software) covers the tools. The main one is
[scikit-fingerprints](https://github.com/MLCIL/scikit-fingerprints), which gives
you over 30 molecular fingerprints through a scikit-learn API. Datasets such as
ApisTox and MolPILE are there too.

[Research](/research) explains what we care about in more detail, including why
we spend so much effort checking whether published results survive a properly
tuned baseline. That question is not specific to chemistry, and neither are we:
some of our work is on sensor data, tabular problems and
[tabletop game design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09196).

Posts here will cover write-ups, releases and whatever else is worth writing
down. If you are a student at AGH looking for a thesis topic, or you use our
tools and something is getting in your way, the [people page](/people) has the
ways to reach us.
